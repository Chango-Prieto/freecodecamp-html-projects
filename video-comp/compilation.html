Top 5 Deftones songs.

Deftones is an Alternative Metal band formed in Sacramento, California, in 1988. There first album (Adrenaline) was release in 1994 and there latest release (Private Music) was in 2024. With 30 plus years of existing they have accumulated a bunch of great tracks, here I´ll list my favorite tracks.

Hole in the Earth

It was the first single from their fifth album Saturday Night Wrist. An explosive album opener that immediately sets the tone of the whole album. Great place to start for a fist time listener.

Diamond Eyes

The first single and track of their sixth album Diamond Eyes. A very strong opener for the album, once again, set the tone for the rest of the album. Catchy melody with heavy riffs. The very 2000's, i love it.

Knife Party

Thier seventh track from their third album White Pony. A track that goes from their aggressive sections to a more slow a quiet ones back to screems and heavy riff.

Be Quiet and Drive (Far Away)

The second single and sixth track of their second album Around the Fur. Very distorted riffs, aggressive vocal, almost shoegaze. Heavy and melodic, as every Deftones track should be.

Digital Bath

The second track of their third album, White Pony. The most known song of the band, and for good reason. It has everything that a great Deftones song have; sweet and melancolic melodies, heavy and distorted riffs, a back and forth of dreamy vibes and aggressiveness and more. My favorite track.
